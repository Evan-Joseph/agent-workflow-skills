# Scene Playbooks

Use these as structure, not canned wording. Replace placeholders with true facts.

## First Contact

Goal: make the receiver understand who is writing, why them, and what action is requested.

Structure:

1. Identity and strongest relevant fact.
2. Concrete reason for contacting this person.
3. One short evidence block showing preparation.
4. One concrete ask.
5. Respectful close with an easy out.

Checklist:

- title/subject identifies request, sender, key reason
- first paragraph fits in 2-4 sentences
- no generic praise that could be sent to anyone
- no more than 1-3 questions

## Help Request

Goal: make helping low-effort.

Structure:

1. “我想请教/麻烦您帮我确认一件具体事情。”
2. State what has already been tried or read.
3. State the exact blocker or decision.
4. Ask for a narrow response: yes/no, direction, example, contact, or next step.
5. Offer a fallback if they are busy.

Avoid apology piles. One respectful phrase is enough.

## Follow-Up After No Reply

Goal: reopen without pressure.

Structure:

1. Briefly reference the previous message and date if useful.
2. Add one new piece of useful information, shorter than the original.
3. Repeat only the core ask.
4. Give a no-pressure exit.

Do not write “您为什么不回复” or imply blame. If 2-3 attempts fail, recommend changing path rather than escalating tone.

## Thanks

Goal: close the loop and reinforce useful help.

Structure:

1. Thank the specific action or advice.
2. Say what it clarified, changed, or enabled.
3. State the next action the sender will take.
4. Close simply.

Avoid long praise. Specific impact is stronger than high adjectives.

## Refusal Or Declining

Goal: decline without burning the relationship.

Structure:

1. Acknowledge the offer, invitation, or effort.
2. State the decision directly and briefly.
3. Give a real reason at the right level of detail.
4. Preserve relationship if true.
5. Close with thanks.

Do not over-explain, invent reasons, or leave the decision ambiguous.

## Apology Or Repair

Goal: own the concrete impact and propose repair.

Structure:

1. Name the specific action or omission.
2. Acknowledge the impact.
3. State what will be changed or repaired.
4. Ask for confirmation only if the receiver needs to decide.

Avoid “如果让您不舒服” when the impact is already clear. Avoid turning the apology into self-defense.

## Conflict Or Correction

Goal: lower heat while keeping the issue clear.

Structure:

1. Start from observable facts.
2. State the impact or concern.
3. Name the shared goal or fair criterion.
4. Propose one next step.

Do not diagnose motives, label character, or stack rhetorical questions. If the other side is emotional, first reflect the practical concern, then move to facts.

## Maintainer, Customer, Or Community Reply

Goal: be useful, factual, and non-defensive.

Structure:

1. Acknowledge the report or concern.
2. State what has been verified.
3. State what is unknown or pending.
4. Give the next step or what evidence would help.
5. Avoid promises beyond current evidence.

For public replies, do not add private details, blame users, or write customer-service fog.
