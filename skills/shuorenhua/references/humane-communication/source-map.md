## Risk Boundaries

- Treat psychology and influence material as relationship-aware communication aids, not manipulation.
- Do not invent facts, credentials, offers, deadlines, or emotional states.
- Do not imply legal, medical, admissions, hiring, or contractual certainty.
- Do not auto-send external messages; draft locally unless the user explicitly authorizes the external action.
- For official email, application, or platform messages, preserve factual evidence and exact names.
