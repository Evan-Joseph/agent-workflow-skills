# Xiaohongshu Engagement Notes

These thresholds are working heuristics for research triage, not platform facts.

## Ratios

Collect/Like:

- `> 40%`: reference-heavy content. Usually tutorials, checklists, templates, resources, or workflows people want to reuse.
- `20-40%`: insight or planning content. Often useful for topic framing or project positioning.
- `< 20%`: entertainment, opinion, or low-reuse content unless comments show otherwise.

Comment/Like:

- `> 15%`: discussion-heavy. Check comments for real user questions, objections, missing sources, and alternative tools.
- `5-15%`: normal interaction.
- `< 5%`: passive consumption. Still useful if collect ratio is high.

Share/Like:

- `> 10%`: social currency or practical sharing value.

## Sorts

- `popular`: best observed ceiling for a keyword.
- `latest`: current posting velocity and recent wording.
- `general`: platform-ranked blend; use for a broad first pass.

## Forms

- Image-text notes often carry reusable templates, checklists, screenshots, and process cards.
- Video notes often carry process demos, real-life cases, and presentation style references.

## Research cautions

- Xiaohongshu metrics can be stale, hidden, formatted as Chinese units, or unavailable.
- Viral notes may reflect presentation skill more than factual value.
- Always separate "lead found on Xiaohongshu" from "verified fact".
